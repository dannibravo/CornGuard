- Participate in farmer UAT and final demo preparation.
- Keep technical documentation synchronized with implementation.

## Ownership Rule

Primary ownership means the named developer is responsible for the module's quality and documentation. It does not mean that other developers may change that module without coordination.

Any change that affects another module's public contract must be reviewed by the owner of the consuming module before merge.
